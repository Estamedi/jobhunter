# Banned Verbs

Source: dabydat/resume-builder-skill (MIT): `bullet-writing-guide.md`, `common-mistakes.md`, `SKILL.md`.

## Weak verbs

- responsible for
- helped
- worked on
- assisted
- handled
- utilized
- participated in
- was involved in

- amplified

- engaged
- engineered
- enhanced
- ensured
- fostered
- headed
- honed
- innovated
- mastered
- orchestrated
- perfected
- pioneered
- revolutionized
- spearheaded
- transformed

- leverage / leveraged
- enhance
- utilize
